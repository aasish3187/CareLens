### 📊 MEMBER 2: The Healthcare Crisis
⏱️ **Time:** `03:00 – 07:30` (4.5 Minutes)
🎯 **Slides on Screen:** **Slide 2 & Slide 3**

#### **Slide 2: The Paper Maze of Indian Healthcare**
* **What Member 2 says:**
  > *"Thank you, [Team Lead]. Judges, let us look at the ground reality of Indian healthcare.*
  > 
  > *Over **78% of all medical records** in India remain completely isolated on physical paper. When an Indian patient visits a doctor, their entire medical history lives in a plastic bag or a disorganized WhatsApp chat.*
  > 
  > *Doctors in overburdened OPDs have barely three minutes per patient. As a result, prescriptions are rushed, doctor handwriting becomes unreadable, and diagnostic reports arrive with dense tables of numbers. The patient leaves the clinic with zero understanding of what is actually happening inside their body."*

#### **Slide 3: Why Patients Suffer**
* **What Member 2 says:**
  > *"Paperwork becomes fear when no one translates the meaning.*
  > 
  > *First, **complex numbers**: Elderly parents see numbers like 'HbA1c 9.2%' or 'Creatinine 1.8' and immediately panic, not knowing if it is manageable or critical.*
  > 
  > *Second, **the brand explosion**: Indian pharmacies sell over **300,000 commercial brand names**. Patients get confused between Glycomet-GP, Janumet, or Amoxyclav, leading to dangerous missed doses.*
  > 
  > *And third, **hopelessness**: When patients don't understand their reports, they either ignore their condition or fall for unverified medical advice on social media.*
  > 
  > *To show you how CareLens completely reimagines this experience, here is **[Name 3]**."*

### 🛡️ MEMBER 4: Technical Engine, Proof Receipts & Medical Safety
⏱️ **Time:** `12:00 – 17:00` (5 Minutes)
🎯 **Slides on Screen:** **Slide 6, Slide 7 & Slide 8** (Now Member 4 has 3 powerful, connected slides!)

#### **Slide 6: How a Document Turns Into Clarity**
* **What Member 4 says:**
  > *"Thank you, [Name 3]. Judges, beneath this friendly interface lies a rigorous 4-step engineering pipeline:*
  > 
  > - ***01. Upload & Clean:** The moment a photo arrives, CareLens straightens tilted angles, removes background shadows, and enhances contrast.*
  > - ***02. Privacy Shield:** Before any processing, our privacy guard masks sensitive personal IDs like Aadhaar numbers or phone numbers.*
  > - ***03. Smart Reading:** Our vision system extracts doctor instructions, medication names, dosages, and test values with high precision.*
  > - ***04. Medicine Normalization:** We match brand names against our database of 300,000 Indian medicines to decode generic salts and meal timings (before or after food)."*

#### **Slide 7: Every AI Claim Has a Receipt**
* **What Member 4 says:**
  > *"Now, here is our proudest engineering breakthrough: **The Split-Screen Verification Studio**.*
  > 
  > *In medical AI, blind trust is dangerous. That is why CareLens operates on a **'Proof Receipt' model**.*
  > 
  > *On our split screen, the original paper sits right beside the extracted insights. When a patient or doctor clicks any extracted fact — such as 'HbA1c 9.2%' — the original document instantly highlights the exact sentence where that fact was found.*
  > 
  > *Every claim has a verifiable receipt. There is zero blind trust."*

#### **Slide 8: Why You Can Trust CareLens (Zero Guesswork)**
* **What Member 4 says:**
  > *"Furthermore, we instituted three strict clinical safety boundaries:*
  > 
  > 1. ***AI Reads, but Code Decides:** The AI only reads the text. Whether a test value is Normal, High, or Critical is determined by our deterministic Python rules engine calibrated against ICMR and NABL medical standards — NEVER guessed by the AI.*
  > 2. ***Never Guess Bad Handwriting:** If doctor handwriting is torn or illegible, CareLens will NEVER guess. It marks the value as 'Needs Review' with a yellow warning tag so the user knows to verify it with their pharmacist.*
  > 3. ***Guide, Never Prescribe:** CareLens clearly explains what reports mean, but we strictly forbid diagnosing disease or prescribing drugs.*
  > 
  > *Now, how do we bring this to 1.4 billion Indians across rural towns and villages? Here is **[Name 5]**."*

*(Member 4 passes to Member 5.)*
