**Slide on Screen:** Slide 6 (The Simple Journey) → Slide 7 (Proof Badges)

**What Teammate 3 Says:**
> *"Thank you, [Name 2]. Judges, to make this work reliably on real Indian documents, we created a seamless 4-step pipeline that requires zero technical effort from the user.*
> 
> *Imagine you are a user. You take a photo of your prescription or lab test with your phone and hit Upload.*
> 
> - ***Step 1 is Instant Privacy & Cleanup:** The moment the photo arrives, CareLens automatically straightens tilted photos, removes shadows, and masks out sensitive personal IDs like Aadhaar numbers or phone numbers before any processing begins. Privacy is built into the foundation.*
> - ***Step 2 is Multi-Layered Vision Reading:** Indian lab reports come in varied layouts — Apollo looks different from Metropolis, which looks different from a local clinic. Our vision intelligence identifies doctor notes, medication tables, test names, and numeric units with pinpoint accuracy.*
> - ***Step 3 is Drug Normalization:** If a doctor writes 'Glycomet-GP 1/500', our system matches it against our database of over 300,000 Indian medicine brands to show the patient its generic composition — Metformin plus Glimepiride — along with when to take it (before or after food).*
> - ***And Step 4 is our proudest innovation: The Split Verification Studio.**
>   Every AI system has one major weakness: people worry about hallucinations. In CareLens, we never ask the user to blindly trust our AI. In our split screen, every single medicine, dose, and lab finding comes with a clickable proof badge. When you click it, the original document highlights the exact sentence where that fact was found.*
> 
> *Every insight has a receipt. And to explain how we ensure total medical safety without AI guessing, I invite **[Name 4]**."*

### ⏱️ SECTION 4: 13:00 – 17:00 | Member 4 (Trust, Safety & Medical Guardrails)
**Slide on Screen:** Slide 8 (Safety First: Zero Guesswork)

**What Teammate 4 Says:**
> *"Thank you, [Name 3]. Judges, in healthcare technology, correctness is not just a feature — it is a life-or-death responsibility.*
> 
> *Many people build AI apps that ask a chatbot: 'Is this blood test normal?' This is dangerous, because generative AI can hallucinate numbers or make up medical advice.*
> 
> *In CareLens, we instituted strict, unbreakable safety boundaries:*
> 
> 1. ***Rule 1: AI Reads, but Code Decides.** The AI's job is solely to transcribe the text. Whether a test value is Normal, High, or Critical is calculated by our deterministic Python rules engine calibrated against standard ICMR and NABL guidelines — never by the AI.*
> 2. ***Rule 2: Never Guess an Unreadable Value.** If a doctor's handwriting is torn or blurred, CareLens will NEVER guess. It marks the value as 'Needs Review' with a yellow warning tag so the patient knows to verify it with their pharmacist.*
> 3. ***Rule 3: Strict Non-Diagnostic Policy.** CareLens clearly tells the patient: 'We explain your report; we do not diagnose disease or prescribe medicine.' It gives them the right questions to ask their doctor.*
> 
> *Because of these strict safety gates, in our automated benchmark testing across Indian clinical documents, our grounding rate is 100% with zero hallucinations.*
> 
> *Now, technology is meaningless if it cannot reach rural and diverse Indian families. To explain our regional inclusion and government ABDM integration, here is **[Name 5]**."*

*(Teammate 4 passes to Teammate 5.)*

## 🛡️ Judges Q&A Defense Matrix (Who Answers What)

Assign questions smoothly so the judges see a **united, cohesive team**:

| Question Category | Lead Responder | 15-Second Winning Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **"What if your AI hallucinates a dose or invents a disease?"** | **Member 4 (Safety Lead)** | *"We strictly forbid the LLM from diagnosing or calculating normal ranges. Abnormal flags are computed by deterministic Python code against ICMR rules. Furthermore, every claim must cite a fact ID from the OCR, and our split screen highlights the exact receipt on the original image."* |
| **"How do you handle unreadable doctor handwriting?"** | **Member 3 (Pipeline Lead)** | *"We use a confidence threshold. If handwriting falls below readability confidence, our system refuses to guess. It outputs `null`, flags the item with a visible 'Needs Review' tag, and tells the user to confirm with their pharmacist."* |
| **"How is this different from pasting a photo into ChatGPT?"** | **Member 2 (Product Lead)** | *"ChatGPT is a conversational black box that often hallucinates, has no grounding receipts, doesn't normalize across 300,000 Indian drug brand names, cannot link to a 3D anatomical twin, and cannot generate ABDM-compliant FHIR R4 clinical bundles."* |
| **"What about patient privacy and data leaks?"** | **Member 3 / Team Lead** | *"We sanitize PII — masking Aadhaar numbers and contact info — before any external API call. We compute document hashes (SHA-256) to cache results without re-uploading, and we log zero raw patient images."* |
| **"How will you scale this to real Indian hospitals and ABDM?"** | **Member 5 (ABDM Lead)** | *"CareLens is architected on standard NRCeS FHIR R4 schemas. The mock ABHA ID and QR code are built to connect with ABDM milestone 1 and 2 gateway APIs as soon as sandbox credentials are integrated."* |
