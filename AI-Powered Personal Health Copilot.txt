FULL CHALLENGE BRIEF
AI-Powered Personal Health Copilot
1. Background
Healthcare information is often fragmented across prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, and diagnostic records, making it difficult for patients to understand and manage their health effectively.

2. Challenge Statement
Design and develop an AI-powered Personal Health Copilot that helps individuals understand, organize, and manage their healthcare journey through intelligent analysis of medical records, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and wellness data.

3. Round 1 Brief
3.1 Core Scope
Medical Record Intelligence & OCR: Ingest prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, and diagnostic records; extract medicines, dosages, test values, diagnoses, and dates.
AI Health Summary in Simple Language: Explain records in plain language, including what abnormal values mean.
Unified Health Profile: Organize everything into one profile or timeline.
3.2 Bonus Features
Multi-language Support: Summaries and interface in at least one regional language (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, etc.); OCR that handles bilingual or handwritten prescriptions.
ABDM/ABHA Readiness: Data model aligned to ABDM standards (FHIR-style records) with a mock ABHA ID link or import. Decide this early, as it shapes the schema.
3.3 Deliverables
Working prototype of upload, extraction, summary, and timeline.
Architecture diagram covering the data pipeline, OCR/AI components, storage, and ABDM-ready schema.
Short demo or a few slides showing the approach.
3.4 Evaluation Criteria — 100 Points
Criterion	Weight	What Judges Look For
AI Utilization	35%	Accuracy of OCR and extraction (medicines, dosages, test values, dates); quality of plain-language summaries and explanations of abnormal values
Technical Architecture	25%	Clean data pipeline, sensible storage, structured data model, ABDM-ready schema
User Experience	20%	Easy upload flow, readable summaries, clear health timeline
Healthcare Impact	10%	Medical correctness, safe wording, no misleading claims, appropriate disclaimers
Presentation & Demo	10%	Working prototype shown clearly in a short demo or a few slides
3.5 Bonus Credit (Round 1)
Multi-language support: regional-language summaries and OCR for bilingual or handwritten prescriptions
ABDM/ABHA readiness: FHIR-style records and a mock ABHA link or import

Bonus credit is added within the criteria above (Multi-language under User Experience, ABDM under Technical Architecture) and capped at about 5 to 10 points in total.
